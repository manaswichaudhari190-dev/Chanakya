Core product philosophy

From relevance to proof.

Chanakya does not stop at finding relevant standards. Every recommendation must satisfy four conditions before it becomes actionable.

🎯

Coverage

Which procurement obligation does the standard satisfy? Every requirement atom must map to at least one verified standard.

REQ → STD
📅

Validity

Was the standard version valid for the relevant tender date? Temporal gate rejects superseded or withdrawn versions.

TEMPORAL GATE
🔗

Closure

Are required relationships and evidence actually verified? A recommendation is actionable only when its proof chain closes.

PROOF CLOSED ✓
⚖️

Minimality

What is the smallest sufficient set of standards? MSSS ensures no redundant standards inflate the recommendation set.

MSSS
NO PROOF → NO ACTIONABLE RECOMMENDATION

This is the hard invariant enforced by the Chanakya proof validator at every pipeline stage.
